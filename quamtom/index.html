How to copy Latitude & Longitude
1) Open your Maps / SAT app. 2) Search your birth place or country. 3) Find the Latitude, Longitude numbers and copy them. 4) Paste where needed in the KP tools. Example format: 7.2862, 80.6316 (or any link that contains @lat,lon).
